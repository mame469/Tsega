Our Story

Tsega Milk began as a small local food and dairy spot in Kazanchis, Addis Ababa, and has grown into a known neighborhood establishment famous for serving fresh dairy and quick meals.
Operates as a popular round-the-clock local eatery in Kazanchis.
Well-regarded by city residents for quick bites, traditional dairy pairings, and accessible local dining.

Tsega Milk is a popular local dairy restaurant and cafe in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, renowned for its fresh dairy products and unique dishes. It operates its own dairy farm, ensuring a farm-to-table experience.
